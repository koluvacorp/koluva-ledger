## English

**Title:** Koluva Ledger

**Summary:** Self-managed personal finance with no account and no cloud: what you can spend today, envelope budgets and a balance forecast. For Windows and Android.

**Description:**
Koluva Ledger is a personal finance app that works with no account, no ads and no connection. Your data is stored only on your device.

What sets it apart:
- **Safe to spend today:** one number. Your free money this month, after scheduled bills and goals, divided by the days left. The math is shown.
- **30, 60 and 90-day balance forecast:** add recurring items once and see the lowest point before it arrives. You are warned if the balance would go negative.
- **Envelope budgeting:** a limit per category, with optional roll-over of what you did not spend.
- **Subscription detector:** finds repeated charges in your transactions and adds up what you pay per month and per year.
- **Debt payoff planner:** compares avalanche and snowball with your numbers and shows how much interest you save.
- **Shared expenses:** who paid, who owes, settle up.
- **Goals with deadlines, a cut-back simulator, a growth calculator and a financial health score.**
- **Multiple currencies, CSV import, AES-256 encrypted backups, a PIN lock, payment reminders.**
- Spanish, English, Portuguese and French. Light and dark themes.
- It updates from inside the app: each download is verified by signature and SHA-256 before installing.

Projections and scores are indicative and are not financial advice.
